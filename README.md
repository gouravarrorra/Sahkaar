SAHKAAR

Cooperative-Owned Digital Service & Workforce Management Platform

SAHKAAR is a cooperative-owned digital platform designed to connect verified skilled workers with households and communities while providing cooperatives with the infrastructure to manage their workforce, services, pricing, welfare, certifications, and government-program coordination.

Unlike conventional private service marketplaces, SAHKAAR is designed around worker participation, transparent governance, consumer protection, and worker welfare.

---

PLATFORM OVERVIEW

SAHKAAR consists of three primary interfaces:

USER APP  
For customers to discover services, request workers, manage bookings, make payments, and access relevant government schemes.

WORKER APP  
For workers to manage their profiles, skills, availability, jobs, certifications, earnings, welfare, insurance, training, and opportunities.

ADMIN PORTAL  
For cooperative administrators to manage workers, services, bookings, pricing governance, welfare, insurance, government programmes, analytics, support, and audit records.

All three interfaces operate through the SAHKAAR backend, which connects to the database and file storage layer.

---

THE PROBLEM

Skilled workers such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, domestic helpers, caregivers, drivers, gardeners, cleaners, and technicians often operate through informal or fragmented systems.

This creates several problems:

• Difficulty finding verified skilled workers  
• Lack of structured worker records  
• Limited access to certification and training  
• Weak digital infrastructure for cooperatives  
• Lack of transparent pricing mechanisms  
• Limited welfare and insurance management  
• Poor visibility into local workforce and skill demand  
• Difficulty connecting workers with relevant government programmes  

At the same time, simply building another private service marketplace does not solve the underlying cooperative-governance problem.

SAHKAAR is designed to address both sides.

Worker participation without monopoly.

Consumer protection without exploitation.

TRANSPARENT GOVERNANCE

SAHKAAR is not simply a booking application.

Its architecture separates participation, decision-making, implementation, and auditing so that important powers are not concentrated in a single actor.

For example, service pricing follows a governed process:

Worker Participation  
↓  
Objective Data  
↓  
Fair Price Range  
↓  
Review  
↓  
Approval  
↓  
Implementation  
↓  
Audit

Workers can participate in pricing decisions, but they cannot unilaterally determine customer-facing prices.

Administrators manage the system but cannot silently bypass the governance rules.

---

GOVERNMENT COORDINATION

SAHKAAR is not a replacement for government systems.

It acts as a coordination layer between cooperatives, workers, and government programmes.

Government Schemes & Benefits  
Government Training Programmes  
Government Workforce Requirements  
↓  
ADMIN PORTAL  
↓  
VERIFIED WORKFORCE

Government schemes and programmes are managed through the Admin Portal and stored in the SAHKAAR database.

Only real, verified, and published scheme records are shown to workers.

SAHKAAR does not generate fictional government schemes.

Final government eligibility, approval, certification, and benefits remain with the concerned government authority.
